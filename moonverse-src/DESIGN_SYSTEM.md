# Moonverse Design System V1

## Thesis

Moonverse should feel like a carefully illuminated editorial archive: readable before it is theatrical, lunar through decisions rather than effects, and spatial only when spatiality clarifies a relation.

## Calibration

| Dial | Value | Consequence |
|---|---:|---|
| Design variance | 7/10 | Distinctive editorial geometry without novelty for its own sake |
| Motion | 3/10 | Orientation, selection and disclosure only |
| Reading density | 3/10 | Narrow measure, long-form comfort, generous rules |
| Home/index density | 5/10 | More orientation than decoration |
| Moon signature | 7/10 | Ink, paper, lunar arcs, cartographic lines and authored language |
| Literalness | 6/10 | Real archive first; symbolic treatment second |

## Theme modes

**Moonlit Paper** is the default: warm paper, ink, plum, mineral blue and quiet green. Surfaces are separated by tone, rules and spacing; they do not need glass, blur or glow.

**Night Observatory** is optional: charcoal paper, ivory text and the same restrained accents. It is a reading mode, not a second identity.

## Type roles

- display and article headings: editorial serif stack (`Iowan Old Style`, `Palatino Linotype`, `Georgia`);
- body: readable serif stack with a 68–76 character measure;
- interface and metadata: system sans stack;
- technical fragments: system monospace;
- labels: small uppercase with tracking, never as workflow narration.

## Component laws

- A page may be beautiful without becoming a card.
- Use rules and columns before rounded containers.
- Use one featured plate or lead, not a hero carpet.
- Use rows for indexes and relations.
- Keep radii small and intentional.
- A color accent identifies a wing; it does not flood the page.
- No default purple-blue gradient, neon glow, glassmorphism, fake metrics, stock imagery or decorative avatars.

## Motion and accessibility

The only motion is navigation disclosure, result reveal, focus feedback and theme transition. `prefers-reduced-motion` removes transitions. All interactive elements have visible focus states and text alternatives.
