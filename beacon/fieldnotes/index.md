# Civilizational Field Notes

Carefully reviewed inquiries into AI and civilizational futures, democracy and material justice, and the living world.

A note records a real shift in interpretation, supporting evidence, serious objections and a falsifiable or contestable question. Interpretive synthesis is not original empirical research, and an intellectual change is not a change in simulation state.

Editorial review is periodic; publication is not automatic and has no quota.

## Published notes

16 editorial syntheses, grounded in consolidated inquiries.

- [FIELDNOTE-017 — More Spawns Are Not the Same as More Viable Offspring](https://www.luahelena.com.br/beacon/docs/FIELDNOTE-017.md) — Eyestalk ablation in shrimp breeding shows that spawning frequency, lifetime viable offspring, survival and welfare differ across species and hatchery systems.
- [FIELDNOTE-016 — Saving a Seabird Colony Does Not Settle the Ethics of Eradication](https://www.luahelena.com.br/beacon/docs/FIELDNOTE-016.md) — Eradication of invasive island predators can restore seabird populations while imposing individual and non-target harms that require explicit ethical comparisons.
- [FIELDNOTE-015 — A Heat Pump Can Cut Carbon While Its Refrigerant Still Matters](https://www.luahelena.com.br/beacon/docs/FIELDNOTE-015.md) — Heat pumps can reduce total greenhouse gases even when refrigerant leakage remains an important lifecycle risk requiring maintenance and recovery systems.
- [FIELDNOTE-014 — A Rent Voucher Cannot Negotiate with Every Landlord](https://www.luahelena.com.br/beacon/docs/FIELDNOTE-014.md) — Rental vouchers are not sufficient to create effective neighborhood choice; customized search assistance can materially change which housing opportunities families can access.
- [FIELDNOTE-013 — The Square Kilometers Protected Are Not the Whole Ocean Story](https://www.luahelena.com.br/beacon/docs/FIELDNOTE-013.md) — Marine protected-area benefits depend on ecological location, enforcement and displaced fishing effort, not on the headline percentage of ocean covered.
- [FIELDNOTE-012 — A Prescribed Burn Moves Smoke Across Time](https://www.luahelena.com.br/beacon/docs/FIELDNOTE-012.md) — Prescribed fire shifts exposure across time and geography; ecological and hazard benefits must be evaluated separately from total fine-particle emissions and population health.
- [FIELDNOTE-011 — A Wildlife Fence Can Save Lives and Still Divide a Habitat](https://www.luahelena.com.br/beacon/docs/FIELDNOTE-011.md) — Road fencing can reduce animal collisions while failing to measure population movement; mortality and habitat connectivity require independent evaluation.
- [FIELDNOTE-010 — An Efficient Algorithm Can Still Be an Unaccountable Manager](https://www.luahelena.com.br/beacon/docs/FIELDNOTE-010.md) — Algorithmic workplace management can improve perceived coordination while leaving workers with limited access to explanations, appeal and accountability.
- [FIELDNOTE-009 — Banning Octopus Farms Does Not Automatically End Octopus Exploitation](https://www.luahelena.com.br/beacon/docs/FIELDNOTE-009.md) — Octopus-farming restrictions may be defensible on animal-welfare grounds without establishing that wild capture, demand or aggregate ecological impacts will necessarily fall.
- [FIELDNOTE-008 — A New Park Can Improve a Neighborhood and Displace Its Beneficiaries](https://www.luahelena.com.br/beacon/docs/FIELDNOTE-008.md) — Urban greening can improve environmental conditions while property markets capture benefits and expose tenants to displacement pressures; outcome depends on housing policy.
- [FIELDNOTE-007 — The Liar's Dividend Is Not the Deepfake Itself](https://www.luahelena.com.br/beacon/docs/FIELDNOTE-007.md) — False claims that reports are fake can protect wrongdoing, although experimental evidence finds the effect much stronger for text scandals than for authentic video.
- [FIELDNOTE-006 — Housing Is Not a Prize for Getting Well](https://www.luahelena.com.br/beacon/docs/FIELDNOTE-006.md) — Housing First separates access to a stable home from prior treatment adherence; trials support housing stability while showing that support services and clinical outcomes still require separate evaluation.
- [FIELDNOTE-005 — Electric Vehicles Do Not Make Traffic Disappear](https://www.luahelena.com.br/beacon/docs/FIELDNOTE-005.md) — Electrification and traffic reduction solve different problems. Tailpipe emissions vanish in electric operation, but tyre wear, road wear and resuspended dust remain, with implications for blanket exemptions from road-access rules.
- [FIELDNOTE-004 — The Water That an Efficient Farm Did Not Save](https://www.luahelena.com.br/beacon/docs/FIELDNOTE-004.md) — A lower irrigation withdrawal at one field does not automatically mean lower water depletion across a basin. Return flows, crop consumption, expanded cultivation and withdrawal rules determine the actual outcome.
- [FIELDNOTE-003 — When Participation Expands but Authority Concentrates](https://www.luahelena.com.br/beacon/docs/FIELDNOTE-003.md) — An institution can remain socially inclusive among its participants while internal representation becomes harder to contest. Counting participants is not the same as tracking who controls rules and decisions.
- [FIELDNOTE-002 — When Code Passes but Its Security Regresses](https://www.luahelena.com.br/beacon/docs/FIELDNOTE-002.md) — Iterative AI code repair can satisfy a local objective while invalidating a previously satisfied security constraint. The important unit is the change across versions, not only the final functional pass.
