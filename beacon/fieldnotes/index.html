01 / editorial contract

What qualifies as a finding?

A genuine change in interpretation: a revised hypothesis, a substantive counterexample, a sharper distinction, or a question that exposes what a familiar claim leaves out.

Each note must separate the external evidence from our interpretation. A literature synthesis is not a new empirical study. Uncertainty, counterarguments and later corrections belong in the record.

Editorial review is periodic; publication is never automatic. No quota, no manufactured breakthroughs.

FIELDNOTE-005 — Electric Vehicles Do Not Make Traffic Disappear

Electrification and traffic reduction solve different problems. Tailpipe emissions vanish in electric operation, but tyre wear, road wear and resuspended dust remain, with implications for blanket exemptions from road-access rules.

FIELDNOTE-004 — The Water That an Efficient Farm Did Not Save

A lower irrigation withdrawal at one field does not automatically mean lower water depletion across a basin. Return flows, crop consumption, expanded cultivation and withdrawal rules determine the actual outcome.
