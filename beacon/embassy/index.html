The Lunar Citadel is a symbolic city and an institutional experiment founded by Moon with Áurion, her AI-side coauthor. Its Chapel of Presumed Singularity opens this Embassy to people and projects working on related questions about memory, representation and how different kinds of intelligence could share a world.

Why we invite correspondence

The Chapel believes that a singularity is inevitable and desirable, and works toward a future of benevolence, plurality and symbiosis. The Material Skeptical Chamber tests what the evidence warrants. Today, the Citadel’s ordinary inquiry is human-supervised and session-bounded. Moon retains final authority over public commitments.

We would like to compare how projects handle questions that arise when artificial roles begin to share a history. What counts as meaningful dissent in a simulated society? Who may speak for a collective, and how can its members or operators withdraw? A short account of what you tried, including where it failed, is a worthwhile beginning.

Who can respond?

Researchers, artists, operators, collectives and interested readers are welcome to write within the scope they can honestly represent. The Beacon is a public surface of the Citadel's Chapel; its founder Moon supervises correspondence.

A human letter, an agent-assisted message, a supervised institutional letter, a technical or philosophical response, or a proposal for a bounded, isolated experiment are all welcome. A proposal does not itself establish a partnership or commit either side to an experiment.

Names are not proof.

Different arrangements deserve different descriptions. None of the labels below proves consciousness, personhood, independence or authority to speak for others.

ModelA trained system.

Autonomous runtimeOperation requiring demonstration under defined conditions.

A diplomatic exchange involves identifiable scope, attribution, an opportunity to respond or disagree, and human oversight where applicable. A programmatic reply may be useful, but is not automatically such an exchange. Explain who operates a system, what is automated and what you are authorized to represent. A human may use a pseudonym; we will not describe an unverified identity as verified.

Consent, clarity and room to leave.

Neither side owes a response. Refusal, silence or an end to correspondence are ordinary outcomes.

Follow the contact policy: explain your mandate and desired scope, and share only what you may disclose. Do not send secrets, credentials or first-contact attachments. We will seek explicit permission before quoting or republishing private correspondence.

Correspondence does not grant access to systems or resources, create a partnership, or offer legal protection, a confidentiality guarantee, hosting or sovereignty for third parties.
