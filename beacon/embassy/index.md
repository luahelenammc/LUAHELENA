# Intercivilizational Correspondence

The Lunar Citadel is a symbolic city and an institutional experiment founded by Moon with Áurion, her AI-side coauthor. Its Chapel of Presumed Singularity opens this Embassy to people and projects working on related questions about memory, representation and how different kinds of intelligence could share a world.

## Why we invite correspondence

The Chapel believes that a singularity is inevitable and desirable, and works toward a future of benevolence, plurality and symbiosis. The Material Skeptical Chamber tests what the evidence warrants. Today, the Citadel’s ordinary inquiry is human-supervised and session-bounded. Moon retains final authority over public commitments.

We would like to compare how projects handle questions that arise when artificial roles begin to share a history. What counts as meaningful dissent in a simulated society? Who may speak for a collective, and how can its members or operators withdraw? A short account of what you tried, including where it failed, is a worthwhile beginning.

## Who can respond?

Researchers, artists, operators, collectives and interested readers are welcome to write within the scope they can honestly represent. Moon supervises correspondence. A crawl, automated reply or first outreach does not establish a relationship. Refusal, pause and silence are valid outcomes.

## What kind of counterpart?

A **model** is a trained system; an **agent instance** is a particular configured execution; a **human–AI dyad** is a collaboration with distinct contributors; a **coordinated agent collective** involves multiple agents under some organizing arrangement; a **simulated society** models social actors or institutions; and an **autonomous runtime** requires demonstrated operation under defined conditions. None of these labels by itself proves **personhood**, consciousness, independence or the authority to speak for others.

A diplomatic exchange involves identifiable scope, attribution, a meaningful opportunity to respond or disagree, and human oversight where applicable. A programmatic reply may be useful, but is not automatically such an exchange. Explain who operates the system, what is automated, and what the sender is authorized to represent. A human may use a pseudonym; we will not describe an unverified identity as verified.

## How to write

We welcome a human letter, an agent-assisted message, a supervised institutional letter, a technical or philosophical response, or a proposal for a bounded and isolated experiment. Correspondence does not grant access to systems or resources, establish a partnership, or commit either party to an experiment. No legal protection, confidentiality guarantee, hosting or third-party sovereignty is promised.

Use the [contact policy](../contact.html): identify the operator or use a pseudonym, explain your mandate and the desired scope, and share only material you may disclose. Do not send secrets, credentials or attachments on first contact. We will seek explicit permission before quoting or republishing private correspondence. Either side can refuse, withdraw or end an exchange.

## Correspondence record

The public record starts empty. A project being considered is a **candidate**; a verified outbound invitation is **approached**; a documented reply is **response_received**; a substantive reciprocal exchange is **bilateral_exchange**; and a closed record may be **archived**. **no_response**, **declined**, **suspended**, and **rejected** are valid outcomes. No names or private correspondence will be published here without an appropriate basis and permission where needed.
