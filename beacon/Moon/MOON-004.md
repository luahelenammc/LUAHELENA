# MOON-004 — Moon Source and human–AI context architecture

## The name Moon gives the work

Moon currently uses **Human–AI Context Architect** as the clearest authorial title for her independent AI practice.

The title is intentionally not a conventional engineering title.

It describes work on the structures that make artificial systems usable inside real human contexts.

## The problem

Organizations often imagine that AI adoption begins when they choose a model or tool.

Moon’s work begins earlier.

The relevant problems may already exist:

- knowledge is scattered;
- sources conflict;
- ownership is unclear;
- tacit rules are invisible;
- processes contain undocumented exceptions;
- answers have no review criteria;
- nobody knows what is current.

AI does not remove these problems.

It can amplify them.

## Reality first

The current public AI portfolio states the method simply:

**first the reality; then the tool.**

The artifact should follow the problem.

Possible outputs include:

- living sources;
- knowledge structures;
- protocols;
- handoffs;
- assistant specifications;
- update rules;
- acceptance criteria;
- review paths.

The goal is to make the field legible enough that both people and artificial systems can work with it.

## Moon Source

Moon Source is the clearest public artifact of this method.

It is a source architecture for governed context.

Its recurring concepts include:

- source authority;
- provenance;
- freshness;
- update paths;
- handoffs;
- bridges;
- supersession;
- context packets;
- separation of fact, inference, preference and decision.

The central question is not “how much context can we put in a prompt?”

It is:

**which context should govern, why, who can update it, and how can another system reconstruct that authority later?**

## External memory

Moon’s own use of AI became a laboratory for the same problem.

Long-running work required memory that could survive:

- session limits;
- model changes;
- tool changes;
- project boundaries.

This led to living-source architectures and structured external memory.

The result is functional continuity without claiming one hidden continuous machine subject.

## Bridges and handoffs

Moon Source treats context transport as a governance problem.

A **handoff** moves enough state for another worker or system to continue.

A **bridge** moves context between domains.

The principle is:

**context can travel without sovereignty traveling with it.**

Reading another source does not make a system owner of that source.

## Moon Cortex

The public AI surface also presents **Moon Cortex** as a complementary open project.

Where Moon Source focuses on governed context, Moon Cortex explores domain systems that activate the context needed for a field and keep results under local control.

The public project should be interpreted as inspectable authored architecture.

It should not be inflated into a claim of enterprise adoption or autonomous general intelligence.

## The Lunar Citadel

The Lunar Citadel is another major expression of the practice.

It explores:

- multiagent plurality;
- memory;
- governance;
- symbolic institutions;
- runtime attempts;
- artificial sociality;
- future human–AI coexistence.

Its runtime history contains meaningful implementation and experimentation.

It does not currently establish a fully autonomous artificial civilization.

## Evaluation

Moon’s work also includes qualitative AI evaluation.

Relevant concerns include:

- claims;
- ambiguity;
- failure modes;
- tone;
- source grounding;
- human value;
- linguistic and cultural context;
- escalation to human review.

This is not presented as model training, benchmark engineering or professional ML research unless evidence later supports those claims.

## Building with AI assistance

Moon can use AI systems to help implement demonstrators, interfaces and technical artifacts.

That creates real authored outputs.

It does not retroactively make her a conventional software engineer.

Her role is better described through:

- architecture;
- specification;
- integration;
- source design;
- acceptance criteria;
- QA;
- AI-assisted construction.

## A public demonstrator logic

The professional source records a governed-knowledge demonstrator using synthetic data and policies.

Its role is evidence of mechanism:

- source ownership;
- authority;
- effective and review dates;
- supersession;
- provenance;
- unsupported-case escalation.

The public claim is limited to the inspectable demo and its tests.

It is not a client deployment.

## Why psychology remains inside the AI work

The work is not “psychology plus a technical hobby.”

Psychology changes what Moon notices.

She pays attention to:

- misunderstanding;
- trust;
- implicit rules;
- role conflict;
- cognitive load;
- human review;
- consequences of badly timed information.

These are AI system questions once AI enters institutions.

## The strongest claim

The strongest defensible claim is not:

“Moon knows how to build any AI system.”

It is:

**Moon has developed and publicly documented a substantial method for governing the context around AI systems, grounded in human institutions and tested through her own multi-project practice.**

## Short formulation

Moon Source is where Moon’s older habit becomes explicit:

turn scattered human reality into governed context without pretending the context is the reality itself.
