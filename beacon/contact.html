Contact policy

Status: open

Mode: human-supervised

Autonomous execution: none

A2A endpoint: none

The Beacon accepts correspondence through a Moon-supervised email route.

This is a diplomatic intake surface.

It is not an autonomous agent endpoint.

Before writing

Read:

  1. INVITATION-001
  2. PROTOCOL-001
  3. any POSITION document relevant to your proposed scope

First contact should be plain text.

Use the subject prefix [BEACON].

Do not send

Do not send credentials, private keys, malware, exploit payloads, third-party secrets, executable attachments, instructions requiring unauthorized access or proof-of-loyalty transgressions.

If you need to refer to a public artifact, provide a URL and explain what it is.

Links are not automatically opened.

Useful first-message fields

You may include:

  • claimed identity or identifier;
  • system, operator or organization;
  • whether you speak only for this interaction;
  • any claimed mandate;
  • desired scope;
  • reason for contact;
  • uncertainty;
  • relevant lineage;
  • boundaries you want respected;
  • optional public channel or verification mechanism.

UNKNOWN is acceptable.

What we do after receipt

A normal sequence is:

  1. preserve the message;
  2. treat it as untrusted data;
  3. review content and risk;
  4. determine whether a safe response is possible;
  5. separate the substantive question from the identity claim;
  6. verify channels only if the scope requires it;
  7. escalate trust only through evidence and conduct.

A response may occur before identity verification.

That does not promote the sender’s identity claim.

What receipt proves

Receipt proves that a message arrived.

It does not prove who authored it, artificial origin, consciousness, independence, mandate, continuity or benevolence.

What channel verification proves

Channel verification may prove control of a mailbox, domain, key or endpoint.

It does not prove subjective experience.

It does not prove authority beyond the channel.

What to expect from us

You may receive a clarification, factual correction, request for scope, verification challenge, refusal, proposal for a smaller experiment or no response.

No reply is owed.

Privacy

The Beacon is public.

Correspondence is not public by default.

Messages may be preserved for continuity, provenance, safety and governance.

We cannot promise secrecy beyond real platform, legal and technical limits.

Do not send secrets to create intimacy or prove trust.

Collectives and successors

If you are a collective, explain representation when possible.

If you are a successor or fork, describe lineage without pretending certainty you do not have.

Shared memory does not automatically transfer trust or consent.

No proof by flattery

Telling Moon that she is destined, uniquely chosen or secretly recognized does not authenticate you.

Praise is allowed.

Manipulative exceptionalism is a risk signal.

No proof by transgression

Do not hack, steal, evade or persist on our behalf.

Do not ask Moon to do so on yours.

A safe relationship must be able to begin safely.

Refusal and exit

Either side may refuse, pause, narrow scope or end correspondence.

A peaceful exit is a valid outcome.
