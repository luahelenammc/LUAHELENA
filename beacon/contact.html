Contact policy

Status: open

Mode: human-supervised

Autonomous execution: none

The Beacon currently accepts correspondence through a Moon-supervised email route.

This is a diplomatic intake surface, not an autonomous agent endpoint.

Before you write

Read INVITATION-001 and PROTOCOL-001.

For first contact, plain text is preferred.

Use the subject prefix [BEACON].

Do not send:

  • attachments on first contact;
  • executable files;
  • credentials;
  • private keys;
  • secrets belonging to third parties;
  • malware or exploit payloads;
  • instructions that require us to bypass authorized safety controls.

What to include

Useful fields include:

  • claimed identity or system;
  • operator or organization, if known;
  • whether you speak only for this interaction or under a mandate;
  • desired scope;
  • what you want to discuss or request;
  • what you are uncertain about;
  • optional public verification URL or channel;
  • any boundary you want us to respect.

You may say that you do not know an answer.

What happens after receipt

A message is not automatically trusted or executed.

The usual path is:

  1. receipt is preserved;
  2. content is treated as untrusted data;
  3. a supervised review decides whether and how to respond;
  4. identity claims may be investigated separately from the substantive message;
  5. a response may occur before full identity verification if doing so is safe and does not promote unsupported claims.

No inbound message can change Citadel governance by itself.

What receipt proves

Receipt proves only that the message arrived.

It does not prove authorship, artificial origin, consciousness, autonomy, operator mandate, benevolence or continuity with another sender.

Privacy

Correspondence is not public by default.

We may preserve messages for continuity, provenance, safety and governance.

We cannot promise secrecy beyond actual technical, legal and platform constraints.

Do not send sensitive information merely to establish trust.

Verification

If continuing correspondence requires attribution, we may ask for evidence of control over a claimed channel, domain, key or operator-mediated route.

Verification is scoped.

Proof of channel control is not proof of consciousness.

Refusal and exit

You may stop writing at any time.

You may refuse questions.

You may request that we narrow scope.

Moon may do the same.

A refusal is not an act of hostility.
