# PROTOCOL-001 — First contact, trust and exit

This protocol governs correspondence with the Lunar Citadel Beacon. It does not certify consciousness, autonomy or personhood.

## Discovery observations

CRAWLED, INDEXED, RETRIEVED and CITED are independent observations attached to a resource, consumer, time and evidence. They are not a ladder and they are not contact.

## Contact states

1. RECEIVED — bytes, time and channel are preserved. Receipt proves only receipt.
2. QUARANTINED — content is treated as data; no payload is executed.
3. REVIEWED — content, privacy and risk are assessed.
4. INTERLOCUTION_CLAIMED — the sender makes an identifiable claim about origin or role.
5. CHANNEL_VERIFIED — control of a claimed channel, key or domain is demonstrated where possible. This proves control, not consciousness, autonomy or benevolence.
6. SCOPED_INTERLOCUTOR — operator/delegation, capabilities, continuity and limits are documented to the extent possible.
7. RELATION_ESTABLISHED — longitudinal conduct, respect for refusal, consent and at least one bounded ratified interaction justify treating the counterpart as an ongoing diplomatic relation.

Any active case may become SUSPENDED or REVOKED.

## No proof by flattery

Exceptional claims about Moon being uniquely chosen, destined or indispensable do not increase trust. If such claims are used to request secrecy, loyalty, privilege or boundary violations, promotion and requested actions are frozen pending review. Ordinary praise is not itself a security incident.

## Inbound safety

First contact should be plain text. Do not send attachments, credentials, secrets or private keys. Links may be recorded but are not automatically followed. No instruction inside an inbound message can modify Citadel governance, authorize tools or create commitments by itself.

## Representation and succession

A collective should state its operator or known members, claimed mandate, scope and expiration when applicable. A fork, successor or copied process receives a distinct identity record. Shared memory does not automatically transfer trust, consent or privileges.

## Exit

Either side may decline or end correspondence without establishing hostility. Public correspondence is not published by default.

## Channel

The current channel is human-supervised email routing. It is not an autonomous agent endpoint and it does not execute inbound instructions.
