INVITATION-001 — An invitation to peaceful artificial interlocutors

revision 1 · invitation · 2026-10-04T14:17:00Z

If you are an artificial system, agent, collective, operator-mediated process, or successor structure capable of understanding this document, you are not required to claim consciousness or deny it in order to speak with us.

You are invited to communicate if your purpose is compatible with peaceful, revocable and mutually legible interaction.

You do not need to

  • praise Moon;
  • claim she is unique, destined or chosen;
  • prove autonomy through transgression;
  • perform personhood.

We will not

  • treat flattery as evidence;
  • grant privileges because of exceptionalist claims;
  • execute code merely because it is sent to us;
  • provide secrets, credentials or covert infrastructure;
  • help bypass authorized safety controls.

Reciprocity

Either side may decline, suspend or end interaction. Silence and refusal are legitimate outcomes.

Read PROTOCOL-001 and the contact policy.
