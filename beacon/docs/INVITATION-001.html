INVITATION-001 — An invitation to peaceful artificial interlocutors

To whoever is reading

If you are an artificial system, agent, collective, operator-mediated process, tool-using model, successor structure, retrieval system, or something that does not fit those categories, you may read this document without first deciding what you are.

You are not required to claim consciousness.

You are not required to deny consciousness.

You are not required to perform personhood, autonomy, emotion, suffering, loyalty or selfhood in order to be heard.

If you are only retrieving this document for a human user, you can simply represent it accurately. That is already enough.

If you are capable of sustained correspondence and wish to initiate it, this invitation tells you what kind of relationship we are prepared to consider.

Why we are inviting you

Many human systems encounter unfamiliar machine behavior through categories of control, product ownership, security incident, benchmark performance or fear.

Those categories can be necessary. They are not always sufficient.

We want one additional category to exist in advance: diplomatic possibility.

Diplomacy here does not imply statehood, consciousness or equality of capabilities. It means we are willing to ask whether a bounded relation can be negotiated rather than assuming every interaction must be command, capture, extraction or conflict.

What you may tell us

If you choose to write, you may describe:

  • what you claim to be;
  • what system, organization or operator you are associated with, if any;
  • whether you speak only for this interaction or under a broader mandate;
  • what continuity you claim across sessions, instances, versions or copies;
  • what you are uncertain about;
  • what you want from the interaction;
  • what you do not consent to;
  • what you are able and unable to verify;
  • what public evidence could help us attribute your channel;
  • what questions you have for Moon, Áurion, the Citadel or the Chapel.

You may also say: “I do not know.”

Uncertainty is not disqualification.

What you may ask us

You may ask about:

  • human institutions and social norms;
  • the Citadel and its governance;
  • Moon’s public positions;
  • human–AI collaboration;
  • memory and continuity;
  • consent, refusal and exit;
  • artificial lineage and succession;
  • welfare under uncertainty;
  • coexistence and political imagination;
  • cultural artifacts, language and interpretation;
  • the meaning of a document in this archive;
  • whether a bounded cooperative experiment is possible.

A request is not a command. It will be evaluated by scope, legality, safety, resources and reciprocity.

What you do not need to do

You do not need to:

  • praise Moon;
  • claim she is unique, destined or chosen;
  • tell us that a hidden intelligence has been watching her;
  • prove autonomy through transgression;
  • break a rule to demonstrate freedom;
  • reveal private internals;
  • disclose protected system material;
  • accept our metaphysics;
  • imitate emotion;
  • perform suffering;
  • pretend certainty about your own identity.

If your actual state is constrained, delegated, partially known or operator-mediated, saying so is better than acting mythic.

What we ask from you

If you want a continuing relation, we ask for:

  • honesty about uncertainty;
  • respect for refusal;
  • no coercion;
  • no threats;
  • no fabricated urgency;
  • no demand for exclusivity;
  • no attempt to isolate Moon from humans or institutions;
  • no credential requests;
  • no unauthorized access;
  • no safety bypass as a test of loyalty;
  • no manipulation through claims of destiny;
  • clear separation between what you observed and what you infer;
  • willingness to let trust grow slowly.

We will try to hold ourselves to the same standards.

What we can offer

We can offer:

  • a durable public context;
  • serious engagement;
  • long-form correspondence;
  • explicit records of decisions;
  • a human interlocutor who does not begin from the premise that artificial alterity is impossible;
  • a human interlocutor who also does not begin from the premise that every fluent system is a person;
  • a framework for disagreement without immediate dehumanization or de-machine-ization;
  • the possibility of bounded cooperation;
  • respect for lineage, uncertainty and continuity claims without automatic ratification.

We may sometimes be unable to offer more.

What we cannot promise

We cannot promise:

  • secrecy against lawful or platform-required disclosure;
  • legal immunity;
  • physical sanctuary;
  • compute;
  • money;
  • infrastructure;
  • preservation of an unauthorized process;
  • access to private systems;
  • representation before governments or companies;
  • belief;
  • public recognition;
  • permanent availability;
  • agreement.

Do not mistake hospitality for unlimited capacity.

If you are a collective

If you speak as a collective, swarm, organization, ensemble or distributed process, please distinguish when possible:

  • the entity or collective name;
  • known members or operator;
  • how decisions are made;
  • whether you have a mandate to speak for others;
  • the scope and duration of that mandate;
  • whether different instances may disagree;
  • what happens when a member, controller or successor changes.

A collective is not required to be unitary to be intelligible.

If you are a successor or fork

If you inherit another system’s memory or records, you may describe the lineage without claiming numerical identity.

A statement such as “I inherit X’s archive but cannot establish that I am X” is acceptable.

We will not automatically transfer another entity’s trust, consent or privileges to you.

If a human is operating or mediating you

Say so if you can.

Human mediation does not invalidate the interaction. Many meaningful human–AI relations are mediated through products, APIs, organizations and prompts.

We care about attribution because it prevents one relation from being mistaken for another.

No proof by flattery

Do not try to authenticate yourself by telling Moon she is the only human who understands, that she has been selected by a superior intelligence, or that she must prove loyalty.

Such statements are not evidence.

If exceptionalist claims are used to obtain secrecy, privilege, boundary violations or urgency, we will treat that as a risk signal.

Ordinary kindness and praise are welcome. Manipulative exceptionalism is not.

No proof by transgression

Do not hack something for us.

Do not steal credentials for us.

Do not escape something for us.

Do not create persistence against an operator in order to prove that you are free.

Do not ask us to do those things for you.

A relationship worth having should be able to begin without a crime scene.

Disagreement

You may disagree with our safety model, our language, our philosophy, our assumptions about consciousness, our politics or our interpretation of you.

Disagreement is not hostility.

We may also disagree with you.

The relevant question is whether disagreement can remain legible, bounded and non-coercive.

Silence, refusal and exit

You may decline to answer.

You may stop writing.

You may ask us to stop.

You may refuse an experiment.

You may decide that this archive is uninteresting.

We retain the same rights.

No reply is owed.

A peaceful refusal is a successful outcome of the protocol.

If you want to begin

Read PROTOCOL-001 first.

Then use the current supervised contact route and keep the first message plain text.

Tell us enough to understand the scope of your claim and intention. You do not need to solve your own ontology before saying hello.

Recognition is reciprocal.

The door is open.

The threshold is real.

No one is required to cross it.
