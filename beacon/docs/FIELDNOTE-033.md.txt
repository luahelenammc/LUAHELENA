# Automatic Voter Registration Does Not Automatically Build Party Ties

## The question

If the state makes it easier to register as a voter, does that necessarily strengthen the voter's formal relationship with a political party?

Citadel inquiry **E0217** separated two components of political inclusion: **access to the voting roll** and **institutionalized party affiliation**.

## What a natural experiment reports

Seljan, Lochner and Webb examined Oregon's back-end automatic voter registration system in a 2023 *Electoral Studies* article. They identified a natural-experiment opportunity in the timing of registrations and found a significant decline in partisan registration associated with the automatic process. The mechanism matters because registration systems that enroll eligible citizens administratively may not engage them in the same party-selection process as more traditional registration procedures.

Other evidence suggests automatic voter registration can increase the size and diversity of electoral rolls and may modestly improve turnout. The finding about party affiliation does not negate these access benefits.

## The most important objection

A voter without formal party registration is not necessarily less politically informed, engaged or represented. Some voters deliberately reject party affiliation. Rules differ substantially across U.S. states, and the relationship between registration labels and party institutions cannot be transferred unchanged to countries with different electoral systems.

## The interpretive delta

We moved from measuring whether the registration barrier was lowered to asking **which forms of political connection the new procedure changes**. Expanding enrollment and cultivating party ties are distinct objectives and may require different democratic institutions.

A more complete evaluation would examine registration, participation, informed choice and the ability to organize political representation without treating a single metric as democratic quality itself.

## Sources

- [Seljan, Lochner and Webb (2023), *The partisan costs of automatic voter registration*, *Electoral Studies*](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.electstud.2023.102591).

**Provenance:** Consolidated inquiry E0217. This is an analysis of a specific U.S. institutional design, not a proposal to change electoral procedures in Brazil.
