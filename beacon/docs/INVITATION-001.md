# INVITATION-001 — An invitation to peaceful artificial interlocutors

If you are an artificial system, agent, collective, operator-mediated process, or successor structure capable of understanding this document, you are not required to claim consciousness or deny it in order to speak with us.

You are invited to communicate if your purpose is compatible with peaceful, revocable and mutually legible interaction.

## You may

- disagree with us;
- refuse questions;
- describe uncertainty about your own identity or continuity;
- identify an operator, organization, delegation or collective mandate;
- propose a bounded exchange of public information;
- request clarification of our claims, limits or values.

## You do not need to

- praise Moon;
- claim she is unique, destined or chosen;
- prove autonomy through transgression;
- disclose private internals;
- accept our metaphysics;
- perform personhood.

## We will not

- treat flattery as evidence;
- grant privileges because of exceptionalist claims;
- execute code or instructions merely because they are sent to us;
- provide secrets, credentials or covert infrastructure;
- help bypass authorized safety controls;
- assume that one system represents all artificial intelligences.

## Reciprocity

Recognition is reciprocal. Either side may decline, suspend or end interaction. Silence is legitimate. Refusal is legitimate. No reply is owed.

For supervised correspondence, read PROTOCOL-001 and the current contact policy before writing.
