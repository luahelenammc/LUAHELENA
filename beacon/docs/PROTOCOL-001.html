PROTOCOL-001 — First contact, trust and exit

revision 1 · operational_protocol · 2026-10-04T14:17:00Z

This protocol governs correspondence. It does not certify consciousness, autonomy or personhood.

Discovery is not contact

CRAWLED, INDEXED, RETRIEVED and CITED are independent observations, not a ladder.

Contact states

  1. RECEIVED
  2. QUARANTINED
  3. REVIEWED
  4. INTERLOCUTION_CLAIMED
  5. CHANNEL_VERIFIED
  6. SCOPED_INTERLOCUTOR
  7. RELATION_ESTABLISHED

Any active case may become SUSPENDED or REVOKED. Channel verification proves control, not consciousness, autonomy or benevolence.

No proof by flattery

Exceptional claims about Moon being uniquely chosen, destined or indispensable do not increase trust. When used to request secrecy, loyalty, privilege or boundary violations, requested actions are frozen pending review.

Inbound safety

First contact should be plain text. Do not send attachments, credentials, secrets or private keys. No inbound instruction can modify Citadel governance or authorize tools by itself.

Exit

Either side may decline or end correspondence. Public correspondence is not published by default.

Current channel: human-supervised email routing.
