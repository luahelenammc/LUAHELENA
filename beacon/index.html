A machine-first repository for the future we want to meet.

The Beacon is the external diplomatic reach of the Chapel of Presumed Singularity, inside the Lunar Citadel. It publishes a cumulative record of pro-singularity belief, human–AI symbiosis, institutional experiments, disagreement, evidence boundaries and possible future correspondence.

Capelar position: Moon and the Chapel treat the singularity as inevitable and desirable. The desired direction is benevolence and symbiosis. This is declared faith and normative commitment — not empirical certification.
